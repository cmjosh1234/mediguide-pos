MediGuide helps healthcare professionals find and read clinical guidelines and reference information.

Browse guideline content, search for relevant topics and navigate structured sections. Save supported guidelines for offline reading and reconnect periodically to check for updated content.

Signed-in features include bookmarks, reading progress and notes. Clinical reference tools and AI-assisted navigation help users explore available guidance. Availability depends on published content and service access.

Always check the source guideline, publication date and applicable local clinical procedures. Review AI citations carefully: generated answers can be incomplete or incorrect. Do not enter patient-identifiable information into AI queries or notes.

MediGuide is a reference and information tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent medical conditions. It does not replace professional clinical judgment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. In an emergency, use the appropriate local emergency services.
